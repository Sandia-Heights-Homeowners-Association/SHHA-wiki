# Membership requests and tracking

**For residents asking to join and office staff handling membership requests.** Residents: use one of the request routes below or contact the office. Staff: check the person's request and correct property/unit. Website account access, a membership request, the website's Is Member flag and SHS billing are distinct.

**Check existing shared-ownership coverage before treating a household as needing a new membership.** Membership from an actual dues-paying property extends to other properties owned or co-owned by people on that property. It does not pass onward through a person connected only to an inherited property. The website’s Is Member flags do not currently capture this extension. See [inherited membership and the members-only export limitation](/link/46).

## How membership requests reach the office

- A resident submits the [Account Request form](https://sandiahomeowners.org/account/request).
- A resident signs in with a magic link and uses the membership toggle when their address is not marked Member. The form shows **Pending**, and the request appears under **Users → User Queue**.
- A resident contacts the office by phone, email or an in-person visit.

**Important exception:** a new person may move to an address while its old membership is still marked active. The new person will not get the usual join prompt. Ask them to contact the office explicitly about joining; do not treat the old flag as their consent or billing enrollment.

## Check the membership request queue

When a resident requests membership through the Member Area toggle, the form shows **Pending** and the request appears under **Users → User Queue** in the website backend.

1. Sign in to the [website backend](https://sandiahomeowners.org/private) with your approved staff account.
2. Open **Users → User Queue** and review pending requests.
3. Check the resident and property/unit, then follow the current office handling below.

**Check this queue manually from time to time.** Email notification has not been confirmed, so do not rely on an email to alert you. Pending means the request is waiting for review; it does not confirm SHS billing.

## Review and decide a queued request

1. Click the request in **Users → User Queue**. Check the request type, resident and exact property/unit.
2. Look for the red **MEMBERSHIP REQUESTED** notice. It identifies the resident’s request to join.
3. Coordinate with **Sandia Heights Services (SHS)** to initiate billing and update the address to reflect the new membership status, as the form instructs.
4. Under **Your Decision**, choose the appropriate **Approved** or **Denied** option. In the signed-in resident example below, the form says no account decision is required; do not create a duplicate account for that request.
5. When the intended decision is ready to save, choose **Save &amp; Close**. Check the saved request decision and the address’s membership status separately. Do not assume that choosing Approved proves SHS billing started or that the address was updated automatically.

### How this differs from changing Is Member directly

The property record also allows a direct **Is Member** checkbox edit. That is a different interface from the queued request’s explicit Approved/Denied review. A property flag alone does not show that staff reviewed the queued request or coordinated with SHS.

## What is not currently tracked

The queue provides a website decision, but there is no separate recorded follow-up confirming that SHS billing started, and no routine audit or reconciliation against SHS records. The website’s Is Member flag and a saved Approved decision do not establish that SHS received a billing request or started billing.

## Finding information about a new member

Evidence of requests and communications with SHS is in the office inbox. Search the relevant correspondence for the person and property/unit. Review the website’s Users → User Queue request/decision and property record separately from correspondence with SHS. There is no separate recorded follow-up establishing that billing began. A sent request or the Is Member flag alone is not confirmation of SHS billing.

Resident names, request details, billing evidence and follow-up notes belong in approved private systems, not in this public wiki.
