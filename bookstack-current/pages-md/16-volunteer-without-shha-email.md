# Volunteer without SHHA Email

This page is for volunteers who participate using a personal email address. You usually do **not** need a paid Microsoft license.

**Any SHHA volunteer who wants their own SHHA email address may have one.** A personal address such as `first.last@sandiahomeowners.org` is available to volunteers as well as staff. Volunteers may also keep using personal email for committee messages and approved SharePoint guest access. Ask office staff for your preferred option; see [account setup and current license costs](https://wiki.sandiahomeowners.org/books/shha-it-help-guide/page/sop-new-users-mailboxes-groups).

For an email-address request, contact [office staff](mailto:office@sandiahomeowners.org). For setup or access problems, contact [IT Help](mailto:ithelp@sandiahomeowners.org).

**Just received an invitation?** [Welcome to SHHA](https://wiki.sandiahomeowners.org/link/49) shows the invitation, sign-in and email code screens step by step.

## First-Time Setup

1. Look for the SHHA organization invitation. A separate group/Teams message may also arrive; you do not need to wait for two emails.
2. Open the organization invitation email and click **Accept invitation**.
3. Use that same email address when prompted to sign in.
4. Open your committee SharePoint link from the [Quick Links &amp; Directory](/books/shha-it-help-guide/page/quick-links-directory) page.
5. Confirm you receive committee mailing list messages.

## Invitation messages you may receive

### 1) Organization invitation email (expected but confusing)

The subject is usually similar to **"Anna, SHHA invited you to collaborate with SHHA"**.

The email usually includes:

- A warning message telling you to proceed only if you trust the sender and organization
- **From:** Microsoft Invitations on behalf of the inviting organization. Look for SHHA and the name of the person who invited you in the message.
- **Organization:** SHHA
- **Domain:** sandiahomeowners.org

This is the important one. Click **Accept invitation** to activate your access.

After you select **Accept invitation**, sign in and complete any permissions prompt for SHHA. Microsoft may then open an app page. Use the direct links on the [Quick Links &amp; Directory](/books/shha-it-help-guide/page/quick-links-directory) page for useful pages and mailing lists.

### 2) Optional group or Teams message

You may also get an email with a subject like **"You have been added to a team in Microsoft Teams"**.

This is a default Microsoft message and cannot be customized by SHHA.

For external volunteers, we do **not** recommend using Teams for SHHA work. Instead, follow the SharePoint links on the [Quick Links &amp; Directory](/books/shha-it-help-guide/page/quick-links-directory) page for the groups you are part of.

## What You Can Expect

- You can receive committee group email at your personal address.
- You can open SharePoint documents you were granted access to.
- You may see references to Microsoft tools SHHA does not use (safe to ignore).
- You may receive Teams-related emails even though your normal workflow is email and SharePoint.

## Most Common Mistakes

- **Not accepting the invitation** — until you do, access will fail.
- **Trying to use Teams** instead of opening the committee SharePoint site in your browser.
- **Using the file’s Share or Copy link controls** instead of sending the committee site address, folder and file name.
- **Sending attachments** when the file belongs in SharePoint.

## Correct File Sharing Pattern

1. Upload the file to the **correct committee SharePoint Documents library**.
2. Use the committee’s **site address from [Quick Links](/link/3)**, then give the folder and exact file name.
3. Email those details to the committee list or intended volunteer. They open the site using the email address already approved for committee access.

**Do not use the file’s Share or Copy link button for external volunteers.** Those file-sharing links do not work for SHHA’s external-volunteer handoff. Follow the [SharePoint screenshot guide](/link/7).

See the [illustrated SharePoint guide](/link/7) for opening, uploading and finding committee files.
