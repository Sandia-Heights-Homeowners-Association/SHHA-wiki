# M365 Email Building Blocks

SHHA uses four distinct patterns for email addresses in Microsoft 365. Before requesting any new address, understand which pattern fits your need. This page is the reference for anyone planning new committees, roles, or special-purpose addresses.

Shared-mailbox licensing details: [Microsoft shared mailbox guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/email/about-shared-mailboxes?view=o365-worldwide).

**Any SHHA volunteer who wants their own SHHA email address may have one.** A personal address such as `first.last@sandiahomeowners.org` is available to volunteers as well as staff. Volunteers may also keep using personal email for committee messages and approved SharePoint guest access. Ask office staff for your preferred option; see [account setup and current license costs](https://wiki.sandiahomeowners.org/books/shha-it-help-guide/page/sop-new-users-mailboxes-groups).

For an email-address request, contact [office staff](mailto:office@sandiahomeowners.org). For setup or access problems, contact [IT Help](mailto:ithelp@sandiahomeowners.org).

## Which Pattern Do I Need?

Walk through these questions when planning a new email address:

1. **Does a volunteer or staff member want their own SHHA email mailbox?**
    - Yes → **Personal mailbox** (requires a license; talk to IT admin)
    - No → continue
2. **Is this one of the six designated officer role inboxes that must survive personnel changes?**
    - Yes → **Shared mailbox**
    - No → continue
3. **Does the address need to deliver to a group of people?**
    - Yes → **Mailing list (Microsoft Group)**
    - No → continue
4. **Do you just need a convenient address that routes to an existing mailbox or group?**
    - Yes → **Alias** on the appropriate existing mailbox or group
    - No → talk to IT admin to figure out the right approach

## Combining Patterns

For a committee or task force, the normal setup is:

<table id="bkmrk-whatpatternexample-f"><thead><tr><th>What</th><th>Pattern</th><th>Typical result</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Committee mailing list</td><td>Microsoft Group</td><td>One address that reaches all current members, including external volunteers</td></tr><tr><td>Committee documents</td><td>SharePoint site</td><td>One group-owned Documents library for the committee record</td></tr><tr><td>Friendly alias (optional)</td><td>Alias on the group</td><td>An additional memorable address that routes to the group</td></tr><tr><td>Officer role inbox (only when applicable)</td><td>Shared mailbox</td><td>One of the six officer addresses; never a general chair or task-force mailbox</td></tr></tbody></table>

You generally do **not** need to create personal mailboxes for volunteers. External volunteers receive group email at their personal addresses (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.) and use SharePoint in a browser.

## Archive Rule

Every mailing list (Microsoft Group) **must** include `admin@sandiahomeowners.org` as a member. This is the archive account that preserves all committee email for continuity and records. It is not monitored for support.
