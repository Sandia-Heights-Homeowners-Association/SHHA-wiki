Quick Reference

Who this is for: staff and volunteers finding an everyday task. Start with Quick Links & Directory for current committee sites, mailing lists and officer addresses.

Common tasks

  • Choose personal email or request an SHHA mailbox.
  • Accept a personal-email invitation.
  • Open your own SHHA mailbox or an approved role mailbox.
  • Email the whole committee.
  • Find, upload or edit committee documents.
  • Find a public utility or an authorized Toolbox task.
  • Follow office setup and handover procedures.

For an email-address request, contact office staff. For setup or access problems, contact IT Help.
