Front Office Calendar (DAKboard)

For office staff and IT support. The front office calendar is a DAKboard display running on a Raspberry Pi 3. Its microSD card was replaced in September 2026.

Manage the display

  • DAKboard control panel — sign in using sandiahomeowners@gmail.com to manage the screen and its content.
  • SHHA Flickr photos — the display pulls its images from this account.

If it stops working

Problem What to do
Screen is blank Check the monitor's power and HDMI input, the HDMI cable, and the Raspberry Pi's power. Restart the Pi.
Pi will not start, freezes, or repeatedly stops displaying Reflash the microSD card with DAKOS using the steps below. If the card fails again, replace it.
Display runs, but photos or content are missing Check the internet connection and the screen's sources in the DAKboard control panel. For images, check the SHHA Flickr source.

Reflash the SD card

  1. Power off the Raspberry Pi and remove its microSD card. Put the card in a reader connected to another computer.
  2. Use Raspberry Pi Imager and follow the official Download and Install DAKOS instructions. Write DAKboard OS (DAKOS) to the card; writing erases the selected card.
  3. Reinsert the card, power on, and follow the on-screen setup to reconnect to the office network and the existing SHHA DAKboard account. Select the existing front office screen.
