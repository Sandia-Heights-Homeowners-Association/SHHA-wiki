Staff

This page is for office staff and operations support volunteers.

When ownership is unchanged, add tenants and other residents manually. Follow Add a resident or tenant manually; do not use New Occupancy for those additions.

Any SHHA volunteer who wants their own SHHA email address may have one. A personal address such as first.last@sandiahomeowners.org is available to volunteers as well as staff. Volunteers may also keep using personal email for committee messages and approved SharePoint guest access. Ask office staff for your preferred option; see account setup and current license costs.

For an email-address request, contact office staff. For setup or access problems, contact IT Help.

For the lobby display, see Front Office Calendar (DAKboard) for sign-in, photos and SD-card recovery.

Website records and welcome visits

For a property ownership change, follow Record a property ownership change — Office guide. It shows the website form, printed GRIT and mailing-list setup, directory consent, and the connection to Welcome Visits.

  • Add a resident or tenant manually — add every resident without recording another ownership change.
  • Add a CSC, ACC or IT website volunteer — Settings → Administrators; IT volunteers use the legacy ITC Lead role.
  • Resident account and preferences guide — explain /account versus /account/request and independent consent settings.
  • Membership requests and tracking — intake routes, SHS billing and the flowchart.

Membership request queue: check Users → Queue in the website backend manually from time to time. Member Area membership requests appear as Pending. Email notification has not been confirmed; do not rely on an email alert. See membership requests and tracking for the current handling and billing limitations.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Ask new volunteers whether they want an SHHA mailbox or prefer personal-email guest access, then follow the matching account procedure.
  • Ensure invitation is accepted before troubleshooting deeper issues.
  • Maintain committee mailing list membership.
  • Coordinate shared role mailbox access changes during transitions.
  • Route technical issues to ITHelp.

Monthly Staff Checklist

  • Review committee membership updates from chairs.
  • Confirm Board reminder workflow is sending as expected.
  • Verify role mailbox access for current officers/chairs.
  • Record unresolved access issues with the account, group or site, exact error, and last successful test. Then check the relevant Microsoft 365 setting before handing the issue to the next person listed in the ownership table.

Add a New Volunteer

  1. Confirm the volunteer’s email choice. Arrange a requested SHHA mailbox through the account SOP, or send a Microsoft guest invitation to the personal email address.
  2. For a guest, confirm invitation acceptance. For an SHHA account, confirm first sign-in and mailbox setup.
  3. Add volunteer to committee mailing list.
  4. Provide direct committee SharePoint link.
See also: SOP: Add a person to a committee mailing list

Shared Role Mailbox Transition

  1. Remove prior role holder's access.
  2. Grant new role holder access.
  3. Grant both Full Access and Send As to the new holder’s own licensed SHHA account. Have them manually add the role mailbox if needed, then test opening, reading and sending from the role address.
See also: SOP: Leadership transition for role inbox

Staff Reminders

  • Most volunteers do not need paid licenses.
  • For committee files, send the committee site address, folder and file name. Follow the SharePoint guide rather than Share or Copy link.
  • Committee files belong in the committee SharePoint Documents library. A copied link does not replace approved access permissions.
