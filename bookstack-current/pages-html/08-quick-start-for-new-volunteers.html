Quick Start for New Volunteers

Who this is for: new SHHA volunteers. First choose which email address you want to use; office staff can help with either option.

Choose your email address

Any SHHA volunteer who wants their own SHHA email address may have one. A personal address such as first.last@sandiahomeowners.org is available to volunteers as well as staff. Volunteers may also keep using personal email for committee messages and approved SharePoint guest access. Ask office staff for your preferred option; see account setup and current license costs.

  1. Keep personal email: ask office staff to invite that address and add your approved committee memberships. Follow the invitation guide and accept the invitation using that address.
  2. Request SHHA email: ask office staff for a personal SHHA mailbox. After setup, follow the SHHA account guide and sign in with your own address.

Check email and committee documents

  1. Open your committee site from Quick Links & Directory. Select Documents; you should see the committee files.
  2. Follow the Committee SharePoint Guide to open or upload a file.
  3. Confirm a committee test message arrives at the address you chose. Send committee-wide messages from your own account to the committee mailing list.

If you also hold an officer role

President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, ACC Chair and CSC Chair have shared role mailboxes. These belong to the position and are separate from a person's own mailbox. Open the role mailbox through your own licensed SHHA account after the office arranges Full Access and Send As. Follow the shared-mailbox instructions and test opening, receiving and sending from the role address.

For an email-address request, contact office staff. For setup or access problems, contact IT Help.
